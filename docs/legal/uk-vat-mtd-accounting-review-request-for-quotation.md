## 1. Request

Website Expert NI invites you to propose a fee for an independent professional review of a UK accounting and HMRC Making Tax Digital for VAT software module.

We are not stating a target price or budget. Please propose your own fee based on the scope below and clearly identify assumptions, exclusions and optional work.

The review will use fictional accounting data and an isolated HMRC sandbox tenant. It does not involve preparing or filing a real taxpayer's VAT Return.

## 3. Scope summary

The review covers:

- chart of accounts and double-entry controls;
- invoices, expenses/purchase invoices, customer and supplier credits;
- receipts, payments, refunds and bank reconciliation;
- journals, reversals, accounting periods and locking;
- standard, reduced, zero-rated, exempt and outside-scope VAT examples;
- service reverse charge, capital purchase and foreign-currency evidence;
- VAT adjustments and correction boundaries;
- Trial Balance, General Ledger, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, management Cash Flow and aged balances;
- VAT Detail and all nine VAT Return boxes;
- immutable VAT review/lock snapshot and accountant evidence pack;
- HMRC sandbox obligations, View VAT Return, liabilities/payments, submission evidence, receipt PDF and failure/reconciliation controls; and
- role permissions and ordinary tenant-isolation checks.
